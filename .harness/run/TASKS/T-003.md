## Description

Tapping a song plays the whole list in order from that song through a Media3 `MediaSessionService` (ExoPlayer,
music audio attributes, audio focus, becoming-noisy pause). A now-playing bar (title, artist, previous,
play/pause, next) sits above the bottom bar; the playing row is highlighted. The Media3 default media
notification appears while playing. The in-app bar and the notification both reach next/previous through the
service's `ForwardingPlayer`, which uses `PlaybackQueuePolicy` (Previous always goes to the previous song).
